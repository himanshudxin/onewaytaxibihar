Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to fulfill your intercity cab booking: your passenger name, mobile phone number, pickup/drop location addresses, and booking schedule. Mobile numbers are verified via one-time SMS/WhatsApp passwords (OTP).

2. Use of Information

Your details are used strictly to: (a) calculate and validate your trip fare, (b) assign and communicate with your commercial chauffeur, (c) dispatch ride confirmation SMS and receipts, and (d) provide customer assistance from our 24x7 Patna dispatch desk.

3. Data Protection & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details with third-party advertisers. All data transmitted through our web application is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Payment processing is executed via RBI-licensed payment gateways; we never store your debit/credit card credentials on our servers.

4. Contact Privacy Officer

For any queries regarding your stored personal data, write to us at privacy@onewaytaxibihar.com or contact our Patna headquarters.
