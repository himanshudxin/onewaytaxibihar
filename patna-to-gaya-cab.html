Patna to Gaya Route & Travel Information

The journey from Patna to Gaya traverses south via NH 22 (Patna - Gaya - Dobhi 4-lane Highway). Key transit towns along the route include Punpun, Masaurhi, Jehanabad, Makhdumpur, and Bela before reaching Gaya town and extending towards the sacred pilgrimage town of Bodh Gaya. The newly expanded highway offers exceptional ride comfort and eliminates the historic bottlenecks.
