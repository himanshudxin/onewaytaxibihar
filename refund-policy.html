2. Refund Method & Timelines

All approved refunds are remitted strictly back to the original source of payment in compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment settlement directives:

  • UPI Transfers (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM): Credits generally appear in your bank account within 2 to 24 hours of dispatch authorization.
  • Netbanking / Credit & Debit Cards: Processed via Razorpay/Cashfree gateway within 3 to 5 banking business days (depending on your issuing bank's clearing cycle).
  • Zero Deduction Guarantee: We do not levy arbitrary "processing fees", "gateway recovery fees", or "administrative retention charges" on valid cancellations.

3. Flight Delay & Airport Fog Protection

Winter fog and air traffic holding patterns are frequent at Patna Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport (PAT) and Darbhanga Airport (DBR). If your inbound flight is delayed or diverted by the airline:

  • You will NOT be charged waiting fees or cancellation penalties.
  • Our central dispatch desk monitors your flight status using the flight number provided during booking and automatically adjusts vehicle staging.

4. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

You can initiate a cancellation through any of the following official channels:

  • Online Passenger Portal: Visit My Bookings, enter your Booking ID and registered mobile number, and tap "Cancel Ride".
  • Direct WhatsApp Helpline: Message our 24x7 Dispatch Desk on WhatsApp (+91 77394 06400) with your Booking ID.
  • Telephone Support: Call our Central Helpline at +91 80021 41816.
  • Email: Write to support@onewaytaxibihar.com with subject "Refund Request - [Booking ID]".
