How It Works

Book your confirmed outstation cab in under 60 seconds.

1

Choose Pickup, Drop & Mobile

Select your pickup and drop towns from our 38-district Bihar directory. Pick your date and time, and enter your 10-digit mobile number. Click CHECK FARE. (No OTP required at this stage).

2

Compare & Select Vehicle

View all-inclusive upfront pricing. Choose between an economical Prime Sedan (Dzire/Etios), spacious Family SUV (Ertiga), or executive Innova Crysta. All fares clearly list fuel, chauffeur fee, and city pickup.

3

Provide Passenger Details

Input passenger name, exact pickup address or landmark, and payment preference (Cash to Driver vs 20% token advance). Review your booking summary.

4

Verify via OTP & Ride Confirmed

Click BOOK NOW to receive an instant 6-digit SMS/WhatsApp OTP. Enter the code to instantly generate your official Booking ID (e.g. OTB-28491). Your chauffeur and car details are dispatched directly to your phone.
