Under Section 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and international privacy standards, every Data Principal (passenger or user) has the statutory right to withdraw consent and request the permanent erasure of their personal information from our databases.

Important Note on Statutory Tax Records: Personal contact information, inquiries, marketing preferences, and draft ride logs are permanently purged within 72 hours. However, under the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 and Indian Income Tax regulations, finalized financial invoices and transaction reference numbers must be retained for the legally mandated statutory audit period.
