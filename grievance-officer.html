Grievance Redressal Escalation Matrix

We hold a zero-tolerance policy towards service deficiencies, overcharging, driver misbehavior, or safety compromises. Our grievance redressal mechanism operates on a transparent 3-tier matrix:

Level 1: 24×7 Operations Desk

For real-time ride tracking, immediate driver coordination, or fare disputes during transit. Call +91 80021 41816 or WhatsApp +91 77394 06400.
Target SLA: Under 30 minutes.

Level 2: Chief Grievance Officer

For unresolved disputes, refund delays, driver safety breaches, or formal consumer grievances under CPA 2019. Email grievance@onewaytaxibihar.com.
Statutory SLA: Acknowledgment within 48 hours; Final Resolution within 15–30 days.

Level 3: Independent Arbitration

If a dispute remains unresolved after 30 days, matters may be referred to sole arbitration in Patna, Bihar under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, or escalated to the National Consumer Helpline (Toll-Free: 1915).
