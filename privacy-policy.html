Your Privacy is Sacred: OneWayTaxiBihar operates on strict data minimization principles. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal passenger information. Your contact details are strictly utilized to coordinate outstation cab dispatches, flight delay tracking, and statutory GST invoicing.

1. Regulatory Compliance & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and erasure of personal data by OneWayTaxiBihar Mobility Solutions ("OneWayTaxiBihar", "we", "us", or "our") when you access our website (onewaytaxibihar.com), booking applications, or telecom helpline.

We comply fully with:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India)
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (Sections 43A and 72A) and IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
  • Google Ads & Merchant Center User Safety & Unacceptable Business Practices Policies

2. What Personal Data We Collect (Data Minimization)

In accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, we collect only the minimum necessary information required to fulfill your transport service contract:

  • Contact Identification: 10-digit mobile number and passenger name (required for chauffeur coordination, OTP verification, and booking lookup).
  • Trip & Telemetry Data: Pickup address, destination city, travel date, pickup time, flight/train numbers (for flight tracking and airport delay mitigation).
  • Billing Information: Company name and GSTIN (strictly voluntary, only if you request a corporate GST Tax Invoice).
  • Payment Reference: Transaction reference IDs (e.g. Razorpay/Cashfree/UPI payment IDs). Note: We do NOT store credit card numbers, CVVs, netbanking credentials, or UPI PINs. All payment processing is directly executed on RBI-licensed payment gateways over PCI-DSS compliant 256-bit SSL connections.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, device type, and anonymized browser user-agent (collected automatically for DDoS defense and server integrity).

3. Purpose of Data Processing (Lawful Basis)

We process your data strictly on the lawful bases of Contractual Necessity (fulfilling your requested ride) and Statutory Compliance:

  • Dispatching verified tourist vehicles and sharing driver name, vehicle number, and contact details 30-45 minutes prior to pickup.
  • Sending real-time transactional ride confirmations, OTP security codes, and live chauffeur tracking links via SMS and WhatsApp.
  • Generating official GST tax invoices for business expense reimbursement.
  • Preventing fraudulent bookings and maintaining automated rate limiters.

4. Zero Third-Party Sale & Controlled Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or trade your data to third-party ad brokers or marketing syndicates. Your data is shared strictly with authorized service processors under contractual confidentiality:

  • Assigned Chauffeur / Driver Partner: Provided passenger pickup location and contact number strictly for ride execution. Chauffeurs are legally prohibited from contacting passengers post trip completion.
  • Telecom Gateways (Fast2SMS / MSG91): Used to deliver instantaneous booking confirmations and OTPs over DLT-registered Indian telecom routes.
  • Authorized Payment Gateways (Razorpay / Cashfree): Securely verifying advance booking token payments.
  • Law Enforcement & Motor Vehicles Department: Disclosed only if formally requested under Section 91 of the CrPC or court order for passenger safety investigations.

5. Age Restriction & Minor Protection (18+ Requirement)

Our transport booking services are intended solely for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors. If a booking is made for a family travel itinerary that includes children, the booking contract must be initiated and authorized by a parent or legal guardian.

6. Data Retention & Right to Erasure (Data Deletion)

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following guaranteed data rights:

  • Right to Access: Review all personal ride records associated with your registered mobile number.
  • Right to Correction: Request immediate correction of any inaccurate booking or invoice details.
  • Right to Erasure / Data Deletion: You may request the permanent removal of your contact information, inquiry history, and stored preferences at any time.

To request permanent deletion of your data, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Portal or email our Grievance Desk at grievance@onewaytaxibihar.com. Requests are completed within 72 hours, except where statutory GST tax retention rules mandate invoice preservation.
