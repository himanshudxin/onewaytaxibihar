Cancellation & Refund Policy

Fair, transparent rules for passenger peace of mind.

1. Free Cancellation (Zero Fee)

Any booking can be cancelled free of charge if requested at least 4 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time. Any advance commitment token paid will be refunded 100% to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

2. Cancellations Within 4 Hours

If a ride is cancelled within 4 hours of the scheduled departure time, or after the driver has already been dispatched towards your pickup address, a nominal convenience fee of ₹250 (to compensate the driver for fuel and time) may be deducted from any advance paid.

3. Service Non-Availability / Technical Cancellation

In the rare event that OneWayTaxiBihar is unable to fulfill a confirmed reservation due to force majeure, highway blockages, or vehicle breakdown, a 100% refund of any prepaid amount will be processed immediately, and our team will endeavor to arrange an alternative partner vehicle.

4. How to Request Cancellation

Simply call our 24x7 helpline at +91 80021 41816 or send a WhatsApp message with your Booking ID to +91 80021 41816. Our dispatch agents will process your request instantly.
