2. Age & Capacity Requirement (18+ Confirmation)

You must be at least 18 years of age and legally capable of entering into binding contracts under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 to create a booking. By submitting a ride request, you warrant and represent that you are 18 years of age or older.

3. Pricing Transparency & Fare Inclusions

We pride ourselves on 100% upfront pricing with zero drip pricing or surprise surcharges:

  • One-Way Booking Guarantee: For all designated one-way trips, you pay only for the journey traveled from origin to destination. Zero return charges or deadhead mileage will be billed to the passenger.
  • What is Included: Fuel charges, vehicle maintenance, driver daily batta / allowance, air conditioning, and statutory GST.
  • Tolls & State Taxes: On fixed-package routes where specified, highway NHAI FASTag tolls are included. On meter-rate bookings, tolls and local municipal parking charges are payable as per actual government toll booth receipts.
  • Baggage Policy: Standard luggage fitting comfortably in the vehicle's designated boot (trunk) is carried at zero additional charge. Commercial cargo or overloading exceeding RTO vehicle weight limits is strictly prohibited.

4. Booking Confirmation & Chauffeur Allocation

  • Advance Token (₹299): For confirmed outstation vehicle reservations, a nominal token of ₹299 may be placed online via UPI/Razorpay to hold the vehicle. The remaining balance is payable directly to the chauffeur upon ride completion via Cash or UPI.
  • Chauffeur & Car Details: Driver contact details, live GPS status, vehicle registration number, and car model will be transmitted via SMS and WhatsApp 30 to 45 minutes prior to your scheduled pickup time.
  • Trip Start OTP: For passenger security and fraud prevention, each ride commences only upon entering or confirming the secure 4-digit ride OTP provided to the passenger's registered phone.

5. Passenger Code of Conduct & Safety

To ensure safety for both passengers and driver partners:

  • Prohibited Substances: Carrying illicit narcotics, unexcised liquor (under the Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act, 2016), firearms, hazardous chemicals, or contraband is strictly forbidden. Chauffeurs reserve the immediate legal right to refuse transport and alert authorities.
  • Seatbelt Mandate: In compliance with the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, front and rear passengers must wear seatbelts at all times during highway journeys.
  • Chauffeur Working Hours & Speed Limits: Chauffeurs must adhere to National Highway speed limits (80-100 km/h) and cannot be compelled by passengers to violate traffic regulations or drive recklessly.
