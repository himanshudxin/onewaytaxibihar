Patna to Darbhanga Route & Travel Guide

The primary corridor from Patna to Darbhanga crosses the Ganges via Mahatma Gandhi Setu onto NH-22 through Hajipur, connecting to the 4-lane NH-27 (East-West Highway Corridor) or via Tajpur - Samastipur. The route provides smooth, high-speed connectivity to key Darbhanga destinations including Darbhanga Airport (DMB), Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Raj Darbhanga Palace, and the Shyama Mai Temple complex.
