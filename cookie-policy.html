1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies include localStorage and sessionStorage in modern HTML5 browsers, which temporarily retain user inputs (such as origin city, destination city, and selected cab tier) so you don't lose your booking data when navigating between pages.

3. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can adjust your cookie settings at any time using our on-page cookie banner. Furthermore, all standard web browsers permit you to block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings $\to$ Privacy and Security $\to$ Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari (iOS/macOS): Settings $\to$ Safari $\to$ Privacy & Security $\to$ Block All Cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings $\to$ Privacy & Security $\to$ Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings $\to$ Cookies and site permissions $\to$ Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Note: If you disable strictly necessary storage, core functionalities such as remembering your selected cab or reviewing booking invoices may require re-entering your details.
