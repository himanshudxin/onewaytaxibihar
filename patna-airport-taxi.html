Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Patna Airport Taxi

Where will my driver meet me at Patna Airport?

Our chauffeur will coordinate with you via phone/WhatsApp upon touchdown and meet you right outside Terminal 1 in the passenger pickup lane or airport parking area with your name card.

Are toll charges and airport parking included in the fare?

Yes, all our confirmed fares are 100% all-inclusive. Tolls, FASTag, driver allowance, and GST are included. No surprise cash demands from drivers.

Can I pay by cash or UPI after reaching my destination?

Yes! You can choose 'Cash on Ride (Zero Advance)' during booking and pay the chauffeur directly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Cash upon safe doorstep arrival.
