Patna to Bhagalpur Route & Travel Information

The journey from Patna to Bhagalpur connects through the eastern agricultural and industrial belt of Bihar. The route proceeds east via the Bakhtiyarpur 4-Lane Expressway onto NH-31 towards Mokama, then links through Munger or Lakhisarai via NH-80 into Bhagalpur. Key destinations served include Bhagalpur Railway Junction, Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur University, the Vikramshila Ancient University ruins, Kahalgaon NTPC, and Sultanganj pilgrimage ghats.
