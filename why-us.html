Zero Return Fare Charged

Traditional offline taxi stands force passengers to pay for round-trip mileage even when they only need to travel one way. With OneWayTaxiBihar, our algorithm pairs return rides, so you pay strictly for one side distance. Save up to 45% on every intercity trip.

Unlike anonymous automated apps, our central operations desk in Patna is staffed 24 hours a day. Whether you need an early morning 4:00 AM airport pickup or a late-night emergency hospital transit, an executive is always on call.
