Frequently Asked Questions

When do I receive chauffeur & vehicle details?
Chauffeur name, mobile number, vehicle model, and registration plate number are dispatched automatically 2 to 4 hours prior to scheduled pickup time via SMS and WhatsApp.
What is the cancellation policy?
100% Free Cancellation! We do not charge any cancellation fee if cancelled up to 2 hours before pickup.

Yes, our outstation fares include toll taxes, GST, and fuel. There are zero return-trip charges or hidden dead-mileage costs.
