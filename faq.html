Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to everything you need to know before booking your ride.

Do I have to pay any return fare for a one-way trip? +
No, absolutely not. You only pay for the one-way distance you travel. Our routing algorithm automatically pairs drivers with return journeys, ensuring you never pay for an empty return car.
Are toll plaza and state tax fees included in the fare? +
Fuel, vehicle hire, chauffeur allowance, and doorstep pickup/drop are completely included. To ensure absolute transparency without adding inflated risk buffers, official Fastag National Highway toll plaza charges and interstate permits (if traveling to UP or Jharkhand) are paid at actuals based on the vehicle's Fastag slip.
When will I receive my driver's contact and vehicle number? +
As soon as your ride is confirmed, our dispatch assigns a nearby chauffeur. Your driver's name, mobile number, vehicle model, and registration plate are sent via SMS and WhatsApp approximately 1 to 2 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time.
What is the cancellation policy? +
Cancellations requested at least 4 hours prior to scheduled pickup time incur zero cancellation fee. If an advance commitment token was paid, it is refunded in full to your original payment mode or credited for your next booking.
Can I pay in cash to the driver? +
Yes! You can choose "Cash to Driver" during booking. No credit card or online payment is required in advance; you can hand over the fare directly to the chauffeur or pay via UPI QR code upon arriving safely at your drop destination.
What if my flight or train into Patna is delayed? +
If you entered your flight number or train number in the "Pickup Note" field, our operations desk monitors the live arrival status. Your driver will adjust their pickup timing accordingly without extra waiting charges up to 45 minutes from arrival.

Still have questions?

Our Patna dispatch team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
