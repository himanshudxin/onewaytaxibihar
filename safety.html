Safety & Chauffeur Standards

Every trip is protected by comprehensive verification, live tracking, and human supervision.

1. Rigorous Chauffeur Screening

Only professional commercial drivers with valid commercial badges and clean driving records are onboarded. Before being dispatched, every driver undergoes:

  • ✓ Bihar RTO commercial driver badge verification
  • ✓ Local police character certificate validation
  • ✓ Minimum 5+ years of state highway night driving experience

2. 24×7 Central Route Monitoring

Our Patna Operations Control Desk tracks every active ride in real-time. If an unexpected halt or detour occurs during an intercity trip, our safety executive immediately checks in with the passenger and driver.

3. Safe Travel for Solo Women & Senior Citizens

We take extraordinary care with late-night hospital visits, exam commutes, and solo travelers. Live trip sharing links can be sent directly to family members via WhatsApp with one tap.

4. Vehicle Fitness & Hygiene Standards

All cabs feature valid comprehensive commercial insurance, active speed governors, RTO fitness certificates, and fully functional AC heating/cooling.
