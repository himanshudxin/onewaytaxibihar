Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Service Description

OneWayTaxiBihar operates an intercity point-to-point commercial taxi dispatch platform connecting travelers with licensed commercial taxi owners and drivers across the state of Bihar, Jharkhand, and adjacent transit corridors.

2. One-Way Fares & Charges

Quoted one-way fares include vehicle hire, fuel, chauffeur fee, and pickup/drop anywhere within municipal city limits. Official highway Fastag toll taxes, state border road taxes (if applicable), and parking tickets incurred during the ride are payable at actuals directly by the customer.

3. Passenger Conduct & Luggage

Passengers must observe lawful behavior. Carrying illegal substances, contraband, or commercial goods exceeding normal baggage space is strictly prohibited. Vehicle capacity limits (4 passengers for Sedan/Hatchback; 6 to 7 for SUV) must be adhered to as per Motor Vehicle Act guidelines.

4. Jurisdictional Authority

Any disputes arising under these terms are governed exclusively by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts located in Patna, Bihar.
