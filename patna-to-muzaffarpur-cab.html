Patna to Muzaffarpur Route Information

The highway connects Patna to North Bihar's commercial capital via the upgraded steel-truss Mahatma Gandhi Setu across the Ganges. Moving north past Hajipur, the route follows the high-speed 4-lane NH 22 through Saraiya and Bhagwanpur directly into Muzaffarpur. Key destinations include Muzaffarpur Junction, Baba Garibnath Mandir, MIT Muzaffarpur, and the Kanti Industrial belt.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any Gandhi Setu bridge toll surcharge?

No. All bridge and highway toll charges are already included in your quoted fare. The driver clears tolls via automated FASTag.

Can I get a pickup from Patna Junction to Muzaffarpur?

Yes, we offer doorstep pickups from Patna Junction, Rajendra Nagar Terminal, Patliputra Junction, or Patna Airport at any hour.

Do you provide return cab options if I need to come back on the same day?

Yes! You can choose Round Trip in the booking form to enjoy a 12% discount on the return leg with waiting time included.
